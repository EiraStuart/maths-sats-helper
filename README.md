# 📐 Maths SATs Helper

A modern, interactive web application to help you and your friends prepare for Maths SATs. Practice problems, take timed quizzes, play engaging games, track your progress, and learn key topics.

## ✨ Features

✏️ **Practice Problems** - Solve 30+ problems with step-by-step explanations, adjustable by difficulty
⏱️ **Timed Quizzes** - Test your speed and accuracy with multiple-choice quizzes (5, 10, or 15 questions)
🎮 **Fun Games** - Math Bingo, Speed Round, Number Challenge, Memory Math
📄 **Past Papers** - Practice real SATs questions from KS2 Standard and KS2 Greater Depth levels
📚 **Topic Learning** - Learn key concepts in 6 major topics with definitions and examples
📊 **Progress Tracking** - Real-time stats and achievements system to keep you motivated
🏆 **Achievements & Rewards** - Unlock badges as you progress (First Steps, Math Master, On Fire!, etc.)
💾 **Data Persistence** - All your progress is automatically saved to your browser

## Topics Covered (Adjustable Difficulty)

- **Algebra** - Solving equations and expressions (Easy, Medium, Hard)
- **Geometry** - Areas, perimeters, shapes, and angles
- **Ratios & Proportions** - Comparing quantities and scaling
- **Statistics** - Mean, median, mode, range, and data analysis
- **Fractions & Decimals** - Operations and conversions
- **Percentages** - Calculations and real-world applications

## 🎮 Games Included

1. **Math Bingo** - Solve problems and mark answers on your bingo card
2. **Speed Round** - Solve as many easy problems as you can in 60 seconds!
3. **Number Challenge** - Guess the mystery number using math clues
4. **Memory Math** - Match questions with their answers (memory game style)

## Getting Started

### Option 1: Using Python (Easiest)
```bash
cd maths-sats-helper
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then open: **http://localhost:8000**

### Option 2: Using Node.js
```bash
cd maths-sats-helper
npx http-server
```

### Option 3: Open Directly
Simply open `index.html` in your web browser (some features may be limited without a server).

## How to Use

### 🏠 Home
- See your overall statistics at a glance
- Quick access to all main features
- View your problem-solving streak

### ✏️ Practice
1. **Select a topic** - Choose a specific topic or "All Topics" for mixed practice
2. **Choose difficulty** - Filter by Easy, Medium, Hard, or see all levels
3. **Solve the problem** - Enter your answer and click "Submit"
4. **Learn from mistakes** - Click "Show Solution" to see step-by-step working
5. **Keep practicing** - Continue with more problems

### ⏱️ Quiz Mode
1. Choose the number of questions (5, 10, or 15)
2. Select difficulty level (Easy, Mixed, or Hard)
3. Click "Start Quiz"
4. Answer multiple-choice questions under time pressure
5. View your score, accuracy, and time taken

### 🎮 Games
Play one of four fun games to keep learning engaging:
- **Math Bingo** - Fast-paced and fun
- **Speed Round** - Race against the clock
- **Number Challenge** - Solve a puzzle
- **Memory Math** - Classic memory game with a twist

### 📄 Past Papers
Practice real SATs questions:
1. Filter by difficulty (Standard or Greater Depth)
2. Solve past paper questions with full explanations
3. Build confidence before the real exam

### 📚 Topics
- Click on any topic to learn key concepts
- Includes definitions, formulas, and worked examples
- Return to practice problems to apply what you've learned

### 📊 Progress
- **🎖️ Achievements** - Unlock badges and celebrate milestones
  - First Steps: Solve your first problem
  - Getting Started: Solve 10 problems
  - Math Master: Solve 50 problems
  - Quiz Champion: Get 100% on a quiz
  - On Fire!: Get 5 correct answers in a row
  - Game Master: Play your first game
  - Polymath: Practice all topics
- **Statistics** - Track your accuracy by topic
- **Best Topic** - See which topic you're strongest in
- **Clear Data** - Reset your progress (careful - can't be undone!)

## Tips for Success

✅ **Practice regularly** - Even 10 minutes a day helps build confidence
✅ **Start with easier problems** - Build momentum and boost confidence
✅ **Read explanations carefully** - Understand the "why" not just the answer
✅ **Take quizzes for speed practice** - Get comfortable with time pressure
✅ **Play games for fun** - Keep learning engaging and enjoyable
✅ **Share with friends** - Compare progress and challenge each other
✅ **Track your achievements** - Celebrate milestones and stay motivated
✅ **Focus on weaker topics** - Use difficulty filtering to target areas needing help
